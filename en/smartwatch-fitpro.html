Smartwatch Sync & Notification Fix

Compatible with Ultra Series, T800, FitPro, and most generic Bluetooth watches.

01

Battery Optimization

On Android, the system often kills background apps to save battery. Go to Settings > Apps > [Your Watch App] > Battery and select "Unrestricted". This ensures the connection stays alive 24/7.

02

Notification Access

Verify that your phone has granted permission to the app. Search for "Notification Access" in your phone settings and make sure the toggle for your watch app is ON.

03

App Side Configuration

Open the app (FitPro, HiWatch, etc.), go to 'Message Push' or 'Notification settings', and manually enable WhatsApp, Instagram, and SMS. Your watch should vibrate on the next incoming message.
